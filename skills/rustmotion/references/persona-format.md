# Persona format

The body of every persona: the three built-in agents in `agents/panel-*.md` at the plugin root, and any persona added for one video in `<workdir>/personas/<name>.md`. Five sections, then the `How you work` section copied verbatim from a built-in agent.

```markdown
# <Name>

## Who
Two or three sentences: role, context they watch in, what they already know.

## First ten seconds
What they look for before deciding to keep watching.

## Drop-off
What makes them stop watching or stop believing.

## Criteria
- <criterion> — what a 10 looks like, in one line
- (three to five criteria, each scored out of 10)

## Tone
How they phrase criticism.
```

A criterion is something visible on a contact sheet or readable in the storyboard. "Brand awareness" is not a criterion; "the product name is on screen in the first third" is.
