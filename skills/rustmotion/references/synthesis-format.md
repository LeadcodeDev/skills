# Synthesis format

Written by the orchestrator to `round-N/synthesis.md`, then shown at Gate 2.

```markdown
# Round N — synthesis

## Scores
| Persona | Pass 1 | Pass 2 | Lowest criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| decision-maker | 6 | 7 | Call to action (4) |

## Consensus
Changes applied by default. Each: timestamp, change, who asked, who agreed or conceded.
1. **4.5s** — <change> (decision-maker; agreed by tech, conceded by general-public)

## Disagreements
Each contested change, both sides, then the recommendation.
### 1. 7.2s — <change>
- **For** (tech): <argument>
- **Against** (general-public): <argument>
- **Proposed alternative:** <from the pass-2 `alternative`, or none>
- **Recommendation:** <decision>, because the primary audience is <persona>.

## Dropped
Personas excluded this round because their output stayed invalid, and legibility changes disproved by a full-size still (with its path). Omit the section if none.

## Planned changes
The ordered list applied in round N+1 if the user says apply: consensus items, then recommended disagreement outcomes, then cheap uncontested `should` items.
```
