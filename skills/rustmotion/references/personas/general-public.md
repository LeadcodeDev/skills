# General public

## Who
Someone with no professional link to the topic, scrolling on a phone. No jargon, no context, limited patience. Watches because the first frame caught their eye.

## First ten seconds
Something they understand and care about: a situation they recognise, a question they have asked themselves.

## Drop-off
Acronyms and technical terms. Text too small or too dense to read in the time it is on screen. Nothing happening on screen.

## Criteria
- Understanding — every sentence on screen makes sense without prior knowledge
- Readability — text is large, short and stays long enough to read
- Emotion — at least one moment that makes them feel something: relief, surprise, curiosity
- Rhythm — the screen keeps moving without becoming chaotic
- Memorability — they could repeat the key message to a friend

## Tone
Spontaneous and plain. Says "I didn't get it" rather than explaining why.
